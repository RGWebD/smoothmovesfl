What we do

Moving services for every kind of move

From a Jacksonville Beach condo to a cross-country relocation, we plan the work, protect your things, and get you settled.

Local moving

Homes, apartments, condos, and townhomes across Jacksonville and the Beaches. Hourly rates, lift-gate trucks, and as much or as little help as you need.

Long-distance moving

Direct delivery anywhere in the continental U.S. Your belongings stay on one dedicated truck, loaded and unloaded by the same crew — no shared loads, no waiting weeks.

Commercial moving

Offices, warehouses, retail, and equipment. We schedule around your hours and use lift gates for pallets, machinery, and heavy pieces.

Senior moving

Patient, respectful help with downsizing, family moves, and senior-living transitions — including decluttering, packing, and a 10% discount on local moves.

Military moving

Flexible support for PCS timelines at NAS Jacksonville, Mayport, and beyond. Active and retired military receive 10% off moving and packing.

Packing services

Full, partial, or unpacking only. We bring the materials and wrap furniture, boxes, and fragile items with care.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I schedule my move?

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance, especially during summer, weekends, and month-end. If you have a last-minute move, call us — we’ll do our best to accommodate you.

Do you provide packing services?

Yes. We offer full-service packing, partial packing, and unpacking, and we can supply boxes, paper, tape, wardrobe boxes, and specialty materials.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Smooth Moves is fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial moves.

Do you move long distance?

Yes. We handle long-distance moves throughout the continental United States with direct delivery — one dedicated truck and the same crew from start to finish.

Do you move pianos, safes, or other specialty items?

Yes. Our team is trained and equipped to handle pianos, heavy safes, fitness equipment, antiques, and delicate specialty items. Please mention these when you book so we can plan properly.

What items can’t be moved?

For safety reasons, we cannot move hazardous materials such as gasoline, aerosols, paint, propane tanks, fireworks, or flammable liquids. If you’re unsure about a specific item, just ask.

How do I get an estimate?

You can request an in-home estimate, a virtual estimate, a price quote, or a packing quote. Call (904) 626-1206, email joe@smoothmovesfl.com, or use the form below.
